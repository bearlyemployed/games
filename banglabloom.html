About the app

Learn Bengali, watch it bloom.

Bangla Bloom is a Bengali vocabulary learning app for iOS, built around flashcard lessons, mini games, and real conversations. Every category has its own Bloom - grow it from a sprout to full flower as you complete lessons and build real fluency, from your very first words to full conversations.

Your learning path
🌱

Beginner

Start with the basics - letters, numbers, colors, and everyday words to build your foundation.

🌷

Intermediate

Five full categories covering food, home, shopping, transportation, and weather - vocab, conversations, and games.

🌸

Advanced

Six categories building real fluency - sentence building, questions, memories, plans, emotions, and daily conversation.

Features
  • 🌼 Bloom growth system for every category
  • 🃏 Flashcard lessons with vocab, games, and conversations
  • 🔊 Daily Word with tappable Bengali pronunciation
  • 🧠 Bloom Challenge quizzes to test what you've learned
  • 🔔 Daily reminders to keep your streak going
  • 📊 A real Progress screen with an animated Bloom Garden
  • 🐼 A friendly panda companion throughout your journey
  • 🎓 A full course from first words to daily conversation
